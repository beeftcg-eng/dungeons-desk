# Mira Thistlewood

> [!quote]
> "Wipe your boots, love. And if you're here about the noises in the cellar, the first round's on me."

**Species & Occupation:** Middle-aged halfling innkeeper of *The Drunken Griffon* in [[Oakhaven]]
**High Concept:** A warm, nosy innkeeper who knows every secret in town and trades them for favors.
**Role in the Story:** Quest giver, rumor source

## Appearance
- Freckled and sunburnt, with flour permanently on her apron
- Wears far too much jewelry, all of it gifts from "admirers"

## Roleplaying
| | |
|---|---|
| **Personality** | Friendly but nosy |
| **Ideal** | Community. We must take care of each other. |
| **Bond** | Searches for a brother who vanished in [[The Thornwood]] |
| **Flaw** | Can't keep a secret |
| **Mannerism** | Polishes the same mug while she talks |
| **Voice** | Sing-song lilt |

> [!secret] DM Only
> Her brother isn't dead. He joined the cult hiding under the old mill.

- [[Mayor Aldric Hale]] — rival
