# Oakhaven

> [!read-aloud]
> Smoke curls from the chimneys of a few dozen timber houses huddled around an enormous, ancient oak. A wooden palisade rings the village, freshly repaired in places.

**Region:** [[The Thornwood]]  ·  **Population:** ~600  ·  **Ruled by:** Mayor Aldric Hale

## Notable Places
1. **The Drunken Griffon**: the inn, run by [[Mira Thistlewood]]
2. **The Great Oak**: a shrine to the old gods
3. **The Old Mill**: abandoned for years. People say it's haunted.
