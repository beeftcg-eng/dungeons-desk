# The Sundering

> [!quote] Archmage Velis, *Fragments of the Fall*
> "The sky split like rotten cloth, and through the wound came the Thornwood."

**Era:** Age of Ash
**Key Figures:** Archmage Velis

## Summary
A magical catastrophe tore open a rift to the Feywild. [[The Thornwood]] grew from the scar in a single night.

> [!secret] The Truth
> The cult under the old mill in [[Oakhaven]] wants to open the rift again.
