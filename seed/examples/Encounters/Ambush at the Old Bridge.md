# Ambush at the Old Bridge

**Location:** [[The Thornwood]], on the road to [[Oakhaven]]
**Difficulty:** Moderate  ·  **Party:** 4 × level 1
**XP Budget:** 300 (4 × 75)

## Read-Aloud
> [!read-aloud]
> The road narrows where it crosses a moss-slick stone bridge. The stream below runs black and quiet. On the far bank, a cart lies on its side, one wheel still turning slowly.

## Creatures
| Creature | Count | CR | XP | Notes |
|---|:--:|:--:|:--:|---|
| [[Goblin Skulker]] | 4 | 1/4 | 50 | Two in the trees, two under the bridge |

## Terrain & Features
- **Lighting:** Dim light under the canopy
- **Cover / Obstacles:** The overturned cart gives half cover
- **Hazards:** The bridge is slippery: DC 10 Dexterity save if a creature Dashes across, or it falls Prone

## Tactics
- The goblins wait until someone checks the cart, then shoot.

## Development
- **If the party wins:** A goblin carries a crude map to the old mill.
- **If they parley:** The goblins say "the hooded ones" pay them in silver.

## Treasure
- 3d6 sp, a Potion of Healing, and the map
