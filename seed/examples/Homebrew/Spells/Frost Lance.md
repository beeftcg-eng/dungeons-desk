# Frost Lance

*Level 2 Evocation · Sorcerer, Wizard*

You hurl a lance of ice at a creature within range. Make a ranged spell attack. On a hit, the target takes 4d8 Cold damage and its Speed is halved until the start of your next turn.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 2.
