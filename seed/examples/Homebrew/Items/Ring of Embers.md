# Ring of Embers

*Ring, rare (requires attunement)*

While wearing this ring, when you deal Fire damage with a spell, you can add 1d6 to one damage roll. Once per Long Rest, you can speak the command word to cast *Burning Hands* (save DC 13) from the ring.
