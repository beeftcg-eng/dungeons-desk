# 1 Notes and the Wiki

## Templates
**+ New note** gives you a template for each kind of D&D content: NPC, Player Character, Monster, Encounter, Location, History, Faction, Session, Homebrew Spell and Homebrew Item. Each one is laid out the way the books do it.

## Macros: type `/`
At the start of any line, type `/` to open the macro menu (or click **＋ Insert**). It has three kinds of macro:
- **Formats**: NPC, character, monster and so on. In an empty note they fill the whole note; otherwise they're added as a section. Shortcuts are **Alt+1** to **Alt+8**.
- **Blocks**: read-aloud box, DM secret, stat block, table, rumor table, image, relationship line, today's in-world date.
- **Roll it now**: a random NPC, a monster at any CR, treasure, or a roll on one of your loot tables.

## Spelling ✔
Misspelled words get a red underline as you type, in **English and Spanish** at the same time. Right-click a word for suggestions, or choose **Add to dictionary** for names like Thorin or Waterdeep so they stop being marked. Pick the languages (or turn it off) in ⚙ Settings → ✔ Spelling. On the phone app, your phone's keyboard does the checking.

## Linking
Type `[[` and choose a note to link it, like [[2 Running Combat]]. A link to a note that doesn't exist yet appears faded, and clicking it creates the note. The panel on the right shows **backlinks**: every note that links here. Add `#tags` anywhere and click one to find every note with that tag.

## Boxes
> [!read-aloud]
> Boxed text you read to the players.

> [!secret] DM Only
> Secrets. These are **never** shown to players, even in a shared note.

## Images
Paste (Ctrl+V) or drag a picture into the editor, or use 🖼 in the toolbar. Images are saved in the **Attachments** folder.

## Version history 🕘
A version of every note is saved automatically every 10 minutes while you type, and again before a note is deleted. Click 🕘 in the toolbar to see old versions and restore one.

## Sharing with players 👥
Click **👥 Share** in the toolbar to show a note in the **Wiki** tab on players' phones, for the whole party or for chosen players. Secret boxes and `%% hidden comments %%` are removed automatically. %% Like this one: players never see it. %%

## Relationships 🕸
Under a `## Relationships` heading, write lines like:

## Relationships
- [[3 Maps and Tokens]] — ally
- [[4 Players' Phones]] — rival

Then click 🕸 in the notes sidebar and switch to **Relationship web**. Green means allies and family, red means enemies and rivals, and blue means "works for" and members.

## In-world dates
Add `world_date: 1024-3-15` to a note's properties and it appears on the **📅 Timeline** and in the calendar.
