# 6 Campaigns, Backups and Updates

## Campaigns 🗺
The button at the top of the sidebar shows the current campaign. Click it to:
- start a **✨ New campaign**
- switch to another campaign
- open any folder as a campaign (even an Obsidian vault)

Each campaign is its own folder in *Documents/Dungeons Desk Campaigns*.

**⇧ Export** (in the campaign list, or **⚙ Settings → Campaign**) saves the whole campaign as one *.ddcampaign.json* file: notes, images, handouts, tokens, maps and the party. **⇩ Import a campaign file…** opens one as a new campaign. That's how a campaign moves between computers, or to and from the phone app.

## The phone app 📱
Dungeons Desk also runs on a phone at **beeftcg-eng.github.io/dungeons-desk**: tap **I'm the DM**, then use your browser's **Add to Home Screen** to install it. It has the same tools for prep and running the table yourself: notes, generators, initiative and encounters, maps, tokens, handouts, loot, the timeline and the rules. The tools sit in a bar along the bottom (swipe it for more), and campaigns are under **⚙ Settings**.
- Campaigns on the phone are saved on that phone. Export one there and import it here (or the other way round) to move it.
- The phone app can't host your players' phones. The LAN game, the QR code and the TV window need this desktop app.
- It works offline once it has been opened, and it updates itself.

## Backups 💾
On this computer, a full copy of the campaign is saved **once a day** in *Documents/Dungeons Desk Backups* (the last 14 are kept). Go to **⚙ Settings → Back up now** to make one any time. Each note also has its own 🕘 version history. On the phone app, back up by exporting a campaign file.

## Themes 🎨
**⚙ Settings → Appearance** has these themes:
- dark: Midnight, Dungeon Stone, Elven Forest, Crimson Keep, Arcane Blue, Feywild, High Contrast
- light: Parchment, Frost

You can also pick your own accent color and a text size.

## Updates ⬆
When a new version comes out, the app tells you. Go to **⚙ Settings → Updates** to download it, then restart. Only the changed parts are downloaded.
