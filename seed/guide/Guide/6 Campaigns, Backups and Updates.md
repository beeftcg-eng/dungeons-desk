# 6 Campaigns, Backups and Updates

## Campaigns 🗺
The button at the top of the sidebar shows the current campaign. Click it to:
- start a **✨ New campaign**
- switch to another campaign
- open any folder as a campaign (even an Obsidian vault)

Each campaign is its own folder in *Documents/Dungeons Desk Campaigns*.

## Backups 💾
A full copy of the campaign is saved **once a day** in *Documents/Dungeons Desk Backups* (the last 14 are kept). Go to **⚙ Settings → Back up now** to make one any time. Each note also has its own 🕘 version history.

## Themes 🎨
**⚙ Settings → Appearance** has these themes:
- dark: Midnight, Dungeon Stone, Elven Forest, Crimson Keep, Arcane Blue, Feywild, High Contrast
- light: Parchment, Frost

You can also pick your own accent color and a text size.

## Updates ⬆
When a new version comes out, the app tells you. Go to **⚙ Settings → Updates** to download it, then restart. Only the changed parts are downloaded.
