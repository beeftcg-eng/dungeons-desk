# 6 Campaigns, Backups and Updates

## Campaigns 🗺
The button at the top of the sidebar shows the current campaign. Click it to:
- start a **✨ New campaign**
- switch to another campaign
- open any folder as a campaign (even an Obsidian vault)

Each campaign is its own folder in *Documents/Dungeons Desk Campaigns*.

**⇧ Export** (in the campaign list, or **⚙ Settings → Campaign**) saves the whole campaign as one *.ddcampaign.json* file: notes, images, handouts, tokens, maps and the party. **⇩ Import a campaign file…** opens one as a new campaign. That's how a campaign moves between computers, or to and from the phone app.

## The phone app 📱
Dungeons Desk also runs on a phone at **beeftcg-eng.github.io/dungeons-desk**: tap **I'm the DM**, then use your browser's **Add to Home Screen** to install it. It has the same tools for prep and running the table yourself: notes, generators, initiative and encounters, maps, tokens, handouts, loot, the timeline and the rules. The bar along the bottom has **🎬 Session**, Notes, Initiative and Map. **⋯ More** has every other tool and your campaigns. In notes, rules and handouts, swipe right from the left edge to go back to the list.
- Campaigns on the phone are saved on that phone. Export one there and import it here (or the other way round) to move it.
- The phone app can't host your players' phones on the Wi-Fi, and has no TV window: those need this desktop app. It can open an **online table** instead (see *4 Players' Phones*).
- **Install it** so it opens like an app: it shows how on the 🎬 Session screen and in ⚙ Settings. On an iPhone, use Share → **Add to Home Screen**, because Safari can clear a website's data after a week or so without a visit. If Chrome says it's **already installed** and offers a shortcut, it is: Android keeps installed apps in the app list even after the home-screen icon is removed, so open **DM Desk** from there.
- It works offline once it has been opened, and it updates itself.
- If a campaign has changed and hasn't been exported for a week, the phone app offers to export it when you open it. Phones sometimes clear app data, so it's worth doing.

## Cloud sync ☁
**⚙ Settings → Cloud sync** keeps a campaign in step between this app and the phone app, or another computer. Sign in with a Pawmodoro account (the same one Beef's Brewhouse uses), or make one right there.
- **☁ Sync this campaign** uploads it: notes, the party, initiative, handouts, maps, tokens and images. After that it syncs by itself every minute or so while the app is open, and before you switch campaigns.
- On another device, sign in and pick it under **In your cloud → ⬇ Get it on this device**.
- **Edited in two places before they synced?** Nothing is lost. A note keeps this device's version, and the other one is saved next to it as *Name (other device date).md*. For the party, initiative, maps and the rest, each part keeps whichever side changed it.
- **Stop syncing** keeps everything where it is. **Delete cloud copy** removes it from the cloud, but every device keeps its own copy.
- **Images** sync both ways. An image deleted on one device (with 🧹 *Clean up images*, below) is deleted on the others too; on this computer it goes to the trash. Images over 10 MB stay on the device they're on, and the app tells you which.
- **Signed in with another account?** The campaign stops syncing until you sign back in with its account, or choose **☁ Sync with this account instead**, which makes a fresh cloud copy in the new one. The character sheet app asks the same way: add the characters to the new account, or remove them from the phone (they stay in the old account).

## Cleaning up images 🧹
Deleting a handout, map or token doesn't delete its picture. **⚙ Settings → Campaign → 🧹 Clean up images** finds pictures no note, handout, map or token uses any more, and animated map videos no map uses, and after asking moves them to the trash (on the phone app, it deletes them). Synced devices delete them too.

## Backups 💾
On this computer, a full copy of the campaign is saved **once a day** in *Documents/Dungeons Desk Backups* (the last 14 are kept). Go to **⚙ Settings → Back up now** to make one any time. Animated map videos are left out, because they're so big: keep the files you exported. Your ★ homebrew library is copied once a day too, into *Documents/Dungeons Desk Backups/★ Homebrew Library* (also 14 kept); to restore a spell or item, copy its note back into a campaign's *Homebrew* folder. Each note also has its own 🕘 version history. On the phone app, back up by exporting a campaign file.

## Themes 🎨
**⚙ Settings → Appearance** has these themes:
- dark: Midnight, Dungeon Stone, Elven Forest, Crimson Keep, Arcane Blue, Feywild, High Contrast
- light: Parchment, Frost

You can also pick your own accent color and a text size.

## Updates ⬆
When a new version comes out, the app tells you. Go to **⚙ Settings → Updates** to download it, then restart. Only the changed parts are downloaded.
