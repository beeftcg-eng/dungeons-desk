# 4 Players' Phones

Players on the same Wi-Fi scan the QR code on the **⏳ Initiative** page (or type the address shown under it). They pick their character, and **a short tour explains everything** the first time.

| Tab | What players get |
|---|---|
| ⏳ Turns | Live turn order. Their phone buzzes on their turn. |
| ✉ Handouts | Your letters and clues, sent from **✉ Handouts → Reveal** |
| 📚 Wiki | Notes you've shared with **👥 Share** |
| 🗺 Map | The map you're showing. They can **move their own token**, **measure** and **ping**. |
| 👤 Me | Their HP, AC, conditions and death saves, a **token maker** for their character's picture, and private notes |

> [!info] What players can and can't do
> Players can only *see* things. The exceptions are their own notes, moving tokens you've assigned to them, pinging, and choosing their token picture. Secrets, hidden monsters and hidden tokens never reach their phones.

**🖥 Open player display window** (on the Initiative page) gives a big-screen view for a TV: the map and the turn order side by side.

> [!warning] Can't connect?
> - The phone has to be on the **same Wi-Fi** as your computer.
> - On **Windows**, allow Dungeons Desk through the firewall when asked (choose *Private networks*).
> - Some public or guest Wi-Fi networks block devices from talking to each other.
