# Start Here: Welcome to Dungeons Desk

Your campaign lives in this **vault**, a folder of plain notes that link together like a wiki. Every tool is in the sidebar on the left.

> [!tip] Start here
> 1. Open **⚙ Settings** and add your party (name, class, level, AC, HP).
> 2. Open **⏳ Initiative** and have your players scan the QR code with their phones. They get a short tour the first time.
> 3. Press **+ New note** (Ctrl+N) or type `/` in any note to drop in a D&D format.

## The guide
Read these notes once and you'll know everything the app can do:

- [[1 Notes and the Wiki]]: links, tags, images, macros, version history, sharing with players
- [[2 Running Combat]]: initiative, the encounter builder, dice, stat blocks
- [[3 Maps and Tokens]]: battle maps, fog of war, measuring, AoE, player tokens
- [[4 Players' Phones]]: what your players see and can do
- [[5 Homebrew and Importing]]: your own monsters, spells and items; importing from D&D Beyond and Open5e
- [[6 Campaigns, Backups and Updates]]: multiple campaigns, backups, themes and updates
